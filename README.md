## What is Toontown Fantasy?
 
Introducing "PLAYER ZER0 STUDIO's Toontown Fantasy", a new kind of Toontown game made by the community, for the community! Our goal is to bring you a brand new genre of Toontown game with heavy focus on storytelling and world building through exploration! Our story takes inspiration from the story from "Disney's Toontown Online" as well as the world of DuckTales (2017) as well as a reworked "Twinkling Shadows - A Toontown Story", it's successor "Twinkling Shadows II: The Wrath of Chadwyk", and others to tell a reimagined take on the beloved Toontown story while keeping it faithful the source material! The fate of the universe is hanging in the balance, so make your Toon then set out on a trilling adventure to save your world from the clutches of The Chairman, the rest of C.O.G.s Incorporated, and beyond!

## Setup:

### Setup - Windows:

1. Make sure you have [git-scm](https://git-scm.com/downloads) installed
2. Locate the place on your computer that you'd like to clone the repository at.
3. Press shift, then right click that folder and open the Command Prompt (or whatever terminal you use.)
4. Type `git clone https://github.com/Arbitrium-Studios/Open-Fantasy.git` and press enter
5. When that's finished cloning, go into the `Open-Fantasy` folder then start `start_game.bat`.
6. Then wait as the game automatically installs everything you need and checks for updates for you before launching the game!
7. That's all!
